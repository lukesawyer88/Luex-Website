How the price works

Who counts as a user, and what changes as you grow.

What counts as a full user?

Anyone who manages, approves or sees the whole picture: owners, managers and admins.

And a light user?

Frontline staff who clock in, fill in forms and read documents. They cost less, and they still use the same app.

Are any tools held back?

No. Every plan includes every tool. There are no feature tiers to upgrade through.

What can cost extra?

Extra storage beyond your allowance, and text messages if you choose to send them. Both are optional.

Before you join

The practical answers.

Launch, pricing, setup and your data, answered plainly.

When does Luex launch?

January 2027. Waitlist members get early access before the public launch.

Do I have to use every tool?

No. You switch on the tools you need at setup and add more later. Nothing you don't need gets in the way.

Can my staff use it on their phones?

Yes. There are apps for phone, tablet and desktop. Staff only see the screens their job needs.

What happens to my data?

It's stored in Ireland, inside the EU, and only people you give access to can see it. You can export everything at any time.

How hard is it to move over?

You can import people, assets and records from Excel or CSV. A setup wizard walks you through the rest, and we help early customers directly.

Is there a contract?

Monthly by default. A 12-month option comes with a lower price.
